How To Play

  • Move with WASD or Arrow keys.
  • Aim with mouse.
  • Left click to attack/use equipped tool.
  • Press E near door/workbench/expansion marker to interact.
  • Gather resources in expeditions and return before time expires.
  • Time Slimes steal time: avoid hits and fight with your sword.
  • Apples restore +3 seconds. At timer zero outside train, you fail.
